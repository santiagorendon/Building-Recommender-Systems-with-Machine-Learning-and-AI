### 3 main points
1. There are other metrics that describe health of recommendations - coverage, diversity and novelty
2. Coverage measures how much of catalog we are using. 
3. Diversity measures how different are recommendations from each other
4. novelty measures if you are recommeding exploratory things. If you show items user always watches and nothing novel they might not discover something new.
