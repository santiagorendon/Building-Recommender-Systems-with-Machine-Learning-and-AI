
# Questions
1. Which metric was used to evaluate the Netflix prize by putting a $1 million bounty on improving a specific metric?

Answer: root mean squared error (RMSE)

2. What's a metric for top-end recommenders that accounts for the rank of the predicted items in truth?

Answer: average reciprocal hit rank 

3. What metric tells us about how popular or obscure our recommendations are?

Answer: novelty 

4. Which metric tells us whether we're recommending the same types of things all the time?

Answer: Diversity

5. Which metric matters more than anything when measuring how real users react to the recommendations?

Answer:  results of online a/b tests
